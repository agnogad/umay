# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in UMAY to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless
of experience, identity, background, or language.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good faith, but accept correction.
- Give technical reasons, not personal ones.
- Keep reviews about the change, not the author.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, publishing private information,
and sustained disruption are not welcome.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space
where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the repository owner,
[@agnogad](https://github.com/agnogad), through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently exclude a participant.

This policy is based on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
